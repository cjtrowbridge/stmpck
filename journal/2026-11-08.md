- Bloom offers a wrapping 24×24 Life grid, paused painting/erasing, single-step, confirmed clear and explicit 2/6/12 generations per second.
- Echo uses four numbered/shaped pads and growing visual sequences up to 32 cues, with saved demonstration/input state and same-sequence retry. Audio is not required or implemented.
- Brick adds three brick layouts, substepped ball collisions, horizontal gestures anywhere, deliberate serves and three lives.
- Planets provides three selectable orbital systems with five to seven planets and zero to two capturable moons. Every player starts on one body with ten units. Orders send all but one immediately to any body, with fixed-speed pursuit of the locked moving destination. Production is one unit per three seconds; combat cancels opposing units one-for-one. Friendly transfers, AI orders and victory account for fleets in transit. Pause and backgrounding stop the simulation; loading never advances offline time.
