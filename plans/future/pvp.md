# Future PvP modes

For [Future PvP modes](../../ROADMAP.md#future-pvp-modes), coordinate player-versus-player support where it improves the game. Linear is computer-only in the current release scope; local two-player play belongs to this future deliverable.

## Scope

Begin with offline local play and shared player/turn/save behavior. Assess Linear, Territory, and Bounce individually. Networking is a separate undecided scope and must preserve offline ordinary play and privacy commitments. Requirements remain unstarted; create a separate plan when each is activated.
